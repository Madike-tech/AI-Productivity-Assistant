AI Productivity Assistant – Project Documentation


1. Introduction

The AI Productivity Assistant is a web-based productivity tool designed to assist users with common administrative and workplace tasks.
The project demonstrates how artificial intelligence concepts and prompt engineering can be applied to practical workplace situations.

2. Project Objectives

The objectives of the project are to:
Automate repetitive administrative activities.
Improve workplace productivity.
Demonstrate practical AI applications.
Apply prompt engineering techniques.
Develop a functional web-based solution.
Demonstrate responsible and ethical AI usage.

3. Features

1.Email Generator
The Email Generator assists users in creating professional workplace emails.
Meeting Summarizer
The Meeting Summarizer helps convert meeting notes into concise summaries and action items.

2.Task Planner
The Task Planner breaks larger activities into smaller and more manageable steps.
Research Assistant
The Research Assistant helps users develop research questions and keywords for a selected topic.

3.AI Chatbot
The chatbot provides general productivity and administrative assistance.

4. AI Tools Used
The project demonstrates the effective use of AI tools such as:
-ChatGPT
-Gemini
-Notion AI
These tools can assist with brainstorming, research, prompt development, testing and improving productivity.

5. Prompt Engineering

The project applies several prompt engineering principles:
-Role definition
-Clear task instructions
-Context
-Specific requirements
-Output formatting
-Constraints
Reviewing and refining AI responses
Structured prompts help AI systems produce more relevant and consistent results.

6. Responsible AI

Responsible AI practices were considered throughout the project.
Users should:

-Avoid entering confidential information.
-Protect personal information.
-Verify AI-generated information.
-Understand that AI can make mistakes.
-Maintain human oversight.
-Use AI ethically and responsibly.
